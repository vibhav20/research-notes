## One-line summary
> Three-pass method for reading Research Papers

## Key claims / contributions
- First pass: general idea,
- Second pass: grasp content of paper (not details),
- Third pass: paper in depth to reimplement paper
- Literature Survey help

## Method
### First Pass:
- Take 5-10 minutes
- Read Title, Abstract, Introduction
- Read Headings of Section and Sub-sections, glance at Maths
- Read Conclusion, glance at references
- Should answer 5Cs:
    - Category of Paper
    - Context of paper
    - Correctness of Assumptions
    - Contributions (majorly)
    - Clarity of paper as a judge
- Decision : using this info decide to read or not read further. (like a judge reviewer of paper)

### Second Pass:
- Upto an hour
- Read in detail (ignore proofs type details)
- Note down things/terms that are complex or questions that come to mind while reading.
- Mark relevant unread references.

- Stop here if paper doesnt lie in your research speciality.

### Third Pass:
- Many hours
- Recreate the work
- Identify and challenge every assumption
- Jot down ideas for future work

- Should be able to reconstruct entire paper from memory.

## How to Literature Review?
- Search for most relevant (cited) content using keywords on search engine 3 to 5 Most Cited
- Use: Google Scholar or CiteSeer
- Do a First Pass on each
- Read related work sections (lucky if you find a recent survey paper)
- Find shared citations across these papers and repeated author names.
- Download key papers, go through researcher's site, identify top conferences.
- Download key papers from these top conferences.
- Make two passes through these papers.
- Check repeated citations and REITERATE
